Team project at SMILES-2026 International Summer School on Machine Learning. Designed and ran grid experiments over number of function evaluations and block size; built the evaluation infrastructure. Reduced generation perplexity from 166 to 49 within 16 sampling steps compared to an autoregressive baseline.
